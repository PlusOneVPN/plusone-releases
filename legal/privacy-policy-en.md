## 2. A short, clear VPN disclosure

Before you use PlusOne VPN or buy a subscription, please understand:

- **Account:** to sign in, we process an email address, Telegram account identifier or Sign in with Apple identifier, depending on your chosen method.
- **Devices:** we process a randomly generated installation/device identifier, platform and operating-system details to supply access keys, enforce device limits and help secure your account.
- **Payments:** we process your subscription status, plan, transaction identifiers and payment confirmation. For App Store purchases, Apple processes your payment credentials; PlusOne receives the purchase/transaction verification information, not your full card number.
- **Optional information:** notifications require a push token and permission; support reports are submitted only by you; crash diagnostics are optional where offered and off by default.
- **VPN activity:** a VPN endpoint technically receives your source IP address to establish the connection. We do not maintain browsing history, contents of your traffic, or lists of websites or DNS queries you visit through the VPN.

**We do not sell user data, use VPN usage data for advertising or profiling, or disclose data about VPN traffic, browsing history or DNS requests to third parties for any purpose. We do not give third parties independent rights to use data obtained through our VPN service.** Essential account, delivery and payment processors described below are used only for the requested service; they must protect data under applicable contracts and law.

The VPN software may temporarily process server selections, connection status and protocol parameters in device memory to make the tunnel work. This is not a record of visited sites. Camera access is used only when you choose to scan a key QR code; the app does not use the QR-camera feature to upload photographs. Clipboard contents are read only when you explicitly ask to paste a key. Split-tunnelling choices are stored locally on the device.

Some infrastructure records, including account login or API security events, can be processed to prevent abuse. We distinguish these technical records from browsing or DNS history and do not use them to profile VPN browsing.

## 4. No traffic logging and third-party restrictions

Our policy is not to retain users' browsing history, DNS query history, visited destination lists or VPN traffic contents. Source IP addresses are technically necessary to deliver network packets while a connection is active. **We do not sell, disclose, transfer or exploit VPN-usage data for advertising, analytics resale, profiling or any third-party purpose.** We do not allow advertising SDKs to inspect VPN traffic.

A VPN is not a guarantee of complete anonymity. Websites you visit and services outside our control may collect information according to their own policies.

## 5. Payment and subscription processing

If you purchase on an Apple device, Apple handles the App Store payment and subscription billing. We receive the signed transaction and status needed to grant access and handle renewals, restorations, revocations and refunds. We do not obtain your complete payment card details.

If you separately use external payment channels outside the iOS app, their payment providers (such as Telegram, Tribute or cryptocurrency payment services where offered) process the information required for those purchases. Their terms and privacy policies also apply. **The iOS app uses Apple's in-app purchase flow for digital subscriptions.**

## 6. Who processes information for us

Only the minimum information required for a requested function may be handled by infrastructure/service providers: hosting and security services for the account backend; email delivery for one-time codes; Apple for Sign in with Apple and StoreKit transactions; Google/Firebase or Apple for push delivery when notifications are enabled; and support tools when you contact us. A Telegram login or support interaction involves Telegram by your choice. We require providers acting on our behalf to protect information and not use it for unrelated purposes.

We do not sell information to advertisers or data brokers. We do not share individual VPN traffic, browsing destinations or DNS histories. We may have to respond to valid legal demands to the extent applicable law requires; we cannot disclose information we do not possess.

## 7. Retention and security

Account identifiers, device records and active subscription entitlements are held while required to provide your account and service. When your account is deleted, active account and device credentials are scheduled for deletion and access keys are revoked as supported by the service; records necessary to meet accounting, tax, anti-fraud or dispute obligations may be retained for their legally applicable periods.

Support requests and voluntarily supplied diagnostics are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the request, maintain security or comply with law, and are deleted or de-identified afterward. Opt-in crash reports can be stopped at any time. Data stored locally on your device can be removed using **Settings → Privacy → Delete app data**. We use technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the data; no transmission or storage method can be guaranteed absolutely secure.

## 8. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability or information about processing, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may withdraw optional consent without affecting processing already carried out lawfully. Contact **rfi@rtk.it.com** for privacy requests.

To delete your account in the app, open **Settings → Privacy → Delete account** and follow the confirmation steps. If account deletion cannot be completed in-app, contact us by email; Telegram-only accounts may need assistance to verify ownership. Deleting an account does not itself cancel an active Apple subscription: cancel it separately in iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

You may complain to the competent supervisory authority, including the Cyprus Commissioner for Personal Data Protection, where applicable.

VPN exit traffic is routed through the location selected by the user or chosen by the service.
