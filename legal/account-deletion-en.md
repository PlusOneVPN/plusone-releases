# Delete your PlusOne VPN Account and Data

**Provider:** RTKIT LTD · Reg. HE430341 · **Privacy contact:** rfi@rtk.it.com.

## Delete your account in the app

1. Open **PlusOne VPN → Settings → Privacy → Delete account**.
2. Read the confirmation about access keys and subscription entitlements.
3. Confirm the request and complete the account ownership verification. For accounts with an email, the app may send a 6-digit verification code.
4. After successful deletion, the app clears its local account/session data. The account and associated active VPN access keys will be removed or revoked as part of the deletion process.

For Telegram-only or other accounts without a working email confirmation method, contact **rfi@rtk.it.com** or https://t.me/plusoneassist so we can verify ownership and assist. Do not send passwords, private VPN keys, identity tokens or payment-card numbers by email.

## If you cannot access the app

Email **rfi@rtk.it.com** with the subject “PlusOne account deletion”. Give only the email address or login method associated with your account and enough non-sensitive details to locate it. We may request a secure ownership check. You may also request a copy of the personal data associated with your account or correction of inaccurate information.

## What happens to information

Active account records and VPN access keys are removed or revoked following verification. Local data can be deleted separately from **Settings → Privacy → Delete app data**. Certain purchase, accounting, security or dispute records may be kept for a period required by applicable law; they will not be used to restore your deleted account. Information previously processed by Apple or another independent provider is governed by that provider's terms.

## Important: cancel Apple billing separately

Deleting your PlusOne account or uninstalling the app **does not cancel an Apple auto-renewable subscription**. On iPhone open **Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions → PlusOne → Cancel Subscription**. For an App Store refund, use https://reportaproblem.apple.com/. Cancel renewal before deleting your account to avoid future charges.
