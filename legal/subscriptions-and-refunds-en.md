# PlusOne VPN — Subscriptions, Renewal, Cancellation and Refunds

**Effective:** 18 November 2026 · **Provider:** RTKIT LTD, Cyprus, HE430341.

## What you are buying

A PlusOne VPN subscription provides access to the VPN service for the selected period, subject to supported device limits and service availability. The plan name, duration, included access and **full total price in your App Store currency** are displayed on the purchase screen before you confirm the transaction. The prices and periods shown by Apple at checkout govern the purchase.

## Automatic renewal (App Store)

- iOS subscriptions are purchased and billed by Apple using In-App Purchase / StoreKit.
- Unless you cancel, your subscription renews automatically for the applicable period at the price shown by Apple, subject to any legally required advance notice of changes.
- Apple charges your Apple Account upon confirmation and handles recurring charges under App Store rules. A trial, if expressly offered on the purchase screen, converts to a paid subscription under the disclosed terms unless canceled before the trial ends.
- To avoid the next renewal charge, cancel at least **24 hours before** the end of the current subscription period, or earlier if Apple's terms specify.
- Cancellation stops future automatic renewals; access usually continues until the current paid period ends, subject to Apple's subscription status.

## How to cancel

On iPhone: **Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions → PlusOne → Cancel Subscription**. You can also manage eligible subscriptions at https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. Deleting the app or deleting your PlusOne VPN account does **not** cancel billing by Apple.

## Restore purchases and accounts

Open the PlusOne app's subscription screen and choose **Restore purchases**, using the same Apple Account that purchased the subscription. You may need to be signed in to the associated PlusOne account. If an Apple subscription is already bound to a different PlusOne account, contact support; we cannot transfer a store transaction between unrelated accounts without appropriate verification.

## Refunds

For purchases through the App Store, Apple decides eligibility and processes refunds. Request one at **https://reportaproblem.apple.com/**. RTKIT LTD cannot directly reverse an Apple App Store payment. A refund or revocation may affect the associated service entitlement. Mandatory consumer rights remain unaffected.

For subscriptions purchased outside the iOS app, contact the original payment channel and our support for the conditions applicable to that transaction.
